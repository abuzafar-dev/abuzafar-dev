## Abuzafar Eshboboyev

I am a backend developer working in Python and Django.

I write the part of a system nobody sees and everybody depends on: the API, the
database schema, and the rules that have to hold when two people do the same
thing at the same second. That last part is what I find genuinely interesting —
a sale rung up twice, a receipt cancelled from two tills at once, a stock count
that drifts because two requests read it before either one wrote. Most of what
I have learned so far came from making those cases impossible rather than
unlikely.

I build for the shops and small businesses around me, in Uzbek and Russian,
which means the software has to work on a cheap phone, on bad Wi-Fi, and in the
hands of someone who has never used a POS before. That constraint has taught me
more about design than any tutorial.

I am looking for a junior backend role where I can keep working on real systems
and learn from people who have already run them in production.
